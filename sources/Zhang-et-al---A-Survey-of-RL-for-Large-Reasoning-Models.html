A Survey of Reinforcement Learning for Large Reasoning Models

清华 + 上海 AI Lab · 配套仓库: TsinghuaC3I/Awesome-RL-for-LRMs

Summary / 摘要

EN: A Tsinghua/Shanghai-AI-Lab survey of RL for Large Reasoning Models (LRMs), focused on the post-DeepSeek-R1 era. Where the Agentic RL survey covers multi-turn decision-making, this one covers single-turn (or short-horizon) reasoning RL — chiefly RLVR: RL with Verifiable Rewards, where programmatic verifiers (answer checkers, unit tests) replace learned reward models. It is organized in three layers: (1) foundational components — reward design, policy optimization, sampling strategy; (2) five foundational debates — Sharpening vs Discovery, RL vs SFT, weak vs strong model priors, tricks vs traps in training recipes, process vs outcome rewards; (3) training resources — static corpora, dynamic environments, RL infrastructure. Its most useful contribution is the debates section: it maps the evidence on each side and shows where the field is converging (hybrid SFT+RL, verifiable rewards, process+outcome combinations).

ZH: 清华与上海 AI Lab 的综述，聚焦 DeepSeek-R1 之后的”大型推理模型”（LRM）的 RL。与 Agentic RL 综述覆盖多轮决策不同，本篇覆盖单轮（或短视界）推理 RL——主要是 RLVR：用程序化验证器（答案检查器、单元测试）替代学习到的奖励模型。全文分三层：(1) 基础组件——奖励设计、策略优化、采样策略；(2) 五个基础争论——磨砺 vs 发现、RL vs SFT、弱 vs 强模型先验、训练配方的技巧 vs 陷阱、过程 vs 结果奖励；(3) 训练资源——静态语料、动态环境、RL 基础设施。最有价值的是争论部分：梳理各方证据并指出收敛方向（SFT+RL 混合、可验证奖励、过程+结果组合）。

Key Takeaways / 核心要点

  • EN: RLVR replaces learned reward models with programmatic verifiers; “Verifier’s law” — tasks with automatic verification (math, code) enable efficient RL, while subjective tasks remain bottlenecked by reward design. ZH: RLVR 用程序化验证器替代学习的奖励模型；“验证器定律”——可自动验证的任务（数学、代码）让 RL 高效可行，主观任务仍卡在奖励设计上。

  • EN: Sharpening vs Discovery: Limit-of-RLVR shows RL raises Pass@1 but hurts large-k Pass@k (sharpening); ProRL shows prolonged RL extends the frontier (discovery). The reconciliation: both happen, and the debate should shift to under what conditions each dominates. ZH: 磨砺 vs 发现：Limit-of-RLVR 表明 RL 提升 Pass@1 却损害大 k 的 Pass@k（磨砺）；ProRL 表明足够长的 RL 能扩展前沿（发现）。和解方案：两者都发生，争论应转向什么条件下谁主导。

  • EN: “SFT memorizes, RL generalizes”: SFT-on-math causes representation drift and catastrophic forgetting of other domains; RL-on-math preserves or enhances them. But RL is no panacea under severe distribution shift — best practice converges on unified/alternating SFT+RL hybrids. ZH: “SFT 记忆，RL 泛化”：数学 SFT 导致表征漂移和对其他域的灾难性遗忘；数学 RL 保持甚至提升其他域。但 RL 在剧烈分布偏移下不是万能药——最佳实践收敛到 SFT+RL 统一/交替混合。

  • EN: Model priors dominate RL outcomes: base models are better RL starting points than instruct models; Qwen improves even under spurious random rewards while Llama/OLMo do not — mid-training (annealing with high-quality math/code data) closes the gap for weak-prior families. ZH: 模型先验主导 RL 结果：基座模型比 instruct 模型更适合做 RL 起点；Qwen 甚至在虚假随机奖励下都能涨点而 Llama/OLMo 不能——中期训练（用高质量数学/代码数据退火）能弥补弱先验家族的差距。

  • EN: Tricks vs traps: decomposing GRPO shows the biggest single gain comes from discarding incorrect samples, not fancy normalization; Clip-Higher (asymmetric PPO clipping) aids exploration. The field’s real problem is inconsistent experimental settings and conflicting conclusions — “scientific training” needs unified protocols and scalability–performance–cost curves. ZH: 技巧 vs 陷阱：分解 GRPO 发现最大单项收益来自丢弃错误样本，而非花哨的归一化；Clip-Higher（非对称 PPO 裁剪）有助探索。领域真正的问题是实验设置不一致、结论互相矛盾——“科学训练”需要统一协议和可扩展性-性能-成本曲线。

  • EN: Outcome rewards give scalable goal alignment but encourage unfaithful CoT (“answer first, hallucinate later”) and reward hacking; process rewards (PRMs) give dense, interpretable guidance but expensive step-wise annotation that generalizes poorly. The promising direction is combining them via implicit process modeling or generative verifiers. ZH: 结果奖励提供可扩展的目标对齐，但鼓励不忠实的 CoT（“先给答案后编理由”）和奖励作弊；过程奖励（PRM）提供稠密可解释的引导，但步骤标注昂贵且泛化差。有前景的方向是通过隐式过程建模或生成式验证器组合两者。

  • EN: Static corpora shift from scale-first to quality- and verifiability-first; small curated sets like LIMO (800 problems) and LIMR punch far above their weight, echoing the data-quality-over-quantity finding elsewhere. ZH: 静态语料从规模优先转向质量与可验证性优先；LIMO（800 题）、LIMR 等小型精选集效果远超体量，再次印证数据质量优于数量。
