对照 · 译文

Weng---Thinking-about-High-Quality-Human-Data#群体的智慧

我的原子笔记

The median of independent crowd guesses converges to the truth

EN — In Galton’s 1907 “Vox populi” experiment, hundreds of fairgoers guessed the weight of a fat ox, and the median guess landed almost exactly on the true weight. Crowdsourcing works when individual errors are independent and unbiased, so they cancel in aggregate; the guarantee breaks the moment guesses are correlated or some participants are spammers optimizing volume rather than accuracy. That is why real annotation pipelines need competence weighting on top of raw aggregation.

中文 — 在 Galton 1907 年的 “Vox populi” 实验中，数百名集市参与者猜测一头肥牛的重量，猜测的中位数几乎恰好等于真实重量。众包有效的前提是个体误差相互独立且无偏，从而在聚合中相互抵消；一旦猜测相互关联，或部分参与者是追求数量而非准确的垃圾标注者，这一保证就会失效。这就是为什么真实的标注流程需要在原始聚合之上再加能力加权。

Weng---Thinking-about-High-Quality-Human-Data#标注者一致性

我的原子笔记

Majority vote assumes equal rater competence so annotators must be weighted by estimated proficiency

EN — Majority vote gives every annotator equal weight, which fails whenever incentives attract spammers who submit random labels to maximize throughput. Proficiency can be estimated from agreement with experts on a gold set, agreement with the rest of the crowd, or probabilistic models like MACE that learn a per-annotator trustworthiness score; aggregation then weights votes by that score. Cohen’s kappa improves on raw agreement by subtracting chance agreement, though it overcorrects when one label class dominates.

中文 — 多数投票给每位标注者相同权重，当激励机制吸引提交随机标签以最大化产量的垃圾标注者时就会失效。熟练度可以通过与专家在黄金集上的一致率、与其他标注者的一致率，或 MACE 这类为每位标注者学习可信度分数的概率模型来估计，聚合时再按分数加权。Cohen’s kappa 在原始一致率中减去随机一致性，是更进一步的指标，但当某一标签类别占主导时会过度修正。

Weng---Thinking-about-High-Quality-Human-Data#标注者分歧与两种范式

我的原子笔记

Rater disagreement is valid signal in subjective tasks not noise to be eliminated

EN — Annotation pipelines usually assume one gold answer and treat disagreement as measurement error. In safety, social, and cultural tasks, multiple interpretations are legitimately correct, and agreement varies from 0.96 on violence to 0.25 on personal topics; Rottger et al.’s descriptive paradigm models the distribution of beliefs instead of forcing a single label. Only disagreement from stochastic errors or poorly designed tasks should be suppressed — the rest reveals who believes what and why, and even annotator identity is a statistically significant factor in toxicity labeling.

中文 — 标注流程通常假设存在唯一黄金答案，把分歧当作测量误差。但在安全、社会、文化类任务中，多种解释可以同时合理正确，一致率从暴力主题的 0.96 到个人主题的 0.25 不等；Rottger 等人的”描述性范式”建模信念的分布，而非强行规定单一标签。只有由随机错误或任务设计不当引起的分歧才应被消除——其余分歧揭示了谁相信什么、为什么，在毒性标注中甚至标注者身份本身也是统计显著的因素。

Weng---Thinking-about-High-Quality-Human-Data#训练过程中的预测变化

我的原子笔记

Training dynamics expose mislabeled data that static filtering misses

EN — After a dataset is built, mislabels surface through how the model trains on them. Data Maps flags samples with persistently low confidence and low variability as likely flipped; AUM exploits the tension between generalization from similar examples and a wrong supervisory signal, which shrinks the training margin; influence functions approximate leave-one-out retraining in closed form to find points whose removal would most reduce their own loss. Training itself becomes a data-quality audit — no clean gold set required.

中文 — 数据集构建完成后，错误标签会通过模型的训练方式暴露出来。Data Maps 将持续低置信度、低变异性的样本标记为可能被翻转；AUM 利用”从相似样本泛化”与”错误监督信号”之间的张力——错标样本的训练 margin 会被这种张力压缩；影响函数以闭式形式近似留一重训练，找出移除后最能降低自身损失的样本。训练本身由此变成数据质量审计——无需干净的黄金集。

Hard-to-learn samples are often the most informative for OOD generalization

EN — Data Maps shows hard-to-learn (low confidence, low variability) samples are the most likely to be mislabeled — but the same study finds ambiguous (high variability) and hard samples improve OOD generalization, beating even training on the full dataset. Treating “hard for the model” as “wrong in the data” would discard the most educational examples. Difficulty is a flag for inspection, not a deletion order.

中文 — Data Maps 表明难学习（低置信度、低变异性）样本最可能被标错——但同一研究发现，模糊（高变异性）和困难样本能改善 OOD 泛化，效果甚至超过用完整数据集训练。把”模型觉得难”当作”数据标错了”会丢弃最有教育价值的样本。困难是需要检查的信号，而非删除指令。

Unforgettable examples can be pruned without hurting model performance

EN — Toneva et al. tracked prediction flips during training and found many examples are “unforgettable”: once classified correctly, never wrong again. These examples are redundant — removing them leaves performance intact — while noisy-labeled and visually unusual examples are the most forgotten. Forgetting events are stable across random seeds and transferable across architectures, making them a reliable, cheap signal for data pruning.

中文 — Toneva 等人追踪训练过程中的预测翻转，发现许多样本是”不可遗忘”的：一旦被正确分类就再也不会出错。这些样本是冗余的——移除它们不影响性能——而带噪声标签和视觉上不常见的样本最容易被遗忘。遗忘事件在不同随机种子间稳定、在不同架构间可迁移，是数据裁剪中可靠且廉价的信号。
